# Relay.green — 1-minute explainer video brief

A self-contained prompt for AI video-generation tools (Runway Gen-4, Pika 2.0,
Sora, Synthesia, HeyGen, Veo). Paste the relevant section into the tool you
choose. The full brief is included so the model has the brand voice, visual
language, and beat-by-beat storyboard it needs to ship a coherent cut.

---

## 1. Audience & purpose

**Who is watching:** A first-time visitor to relay.green. Likely a
non-technical AI builder — a marketing manager prototyping in Lovable, a
founder shipping with Cursor, a clinical operations lead automating with
Claude, an analyst spinning up tools in Replit. They have an idea, they
have an AI, they don't have an engineering team.

**What they need to understand in 60 seconds:**

1. **Why Relay exists** — AI lets anyone build software, but architecture,
   security, deployment, and ongoing care still want a senior engineer.
2. **How it works** — One press, inside the AI tool you're already using,
   puts a real Relay engineer in your session through Zoom (chat, voice,
   or screen).
3. **For what** — Three phases: **Build · Launch & Go-Live · Maintain &
   Scale.** Same team end-to-end.
4. **What it isn't** — Not a freelance marketplace. Not a chatbot. Not a
   ticket queue. A real human, by name and face, on call.

**Positioning line for the cut:** Relay is the best friend of the
non-technical AI builder — the senior engineer they don't have on their
team, available the moment the AI runs out.

---

## 2. Brand & visual language

**Mood:** Editorial. Calm. Confident. Anthropic-adjacent. Think *New York
Times Magazine* meets *Linear*. No tech-bro hype, no swooshes, no
"revolutionize."

**Color palette (use exactly these):**

- Cream `#F4F2EE` — page background, dominant 80% of frames
- Ink `#1A1814` — body text, dark sections, the wordmark itself
- Moss green `#4F6B3A` — the dot. Use sparingly. Reserve for the human
  contact moment.
- Deep moss `#3F5C2E` — italic emphasis, hover states
- Paper `#F9F7F3` — secondary surface for cards
- Rule grey `#D8D2C5` — hairlines, dividers

**Typography (overlay text on screen):**

- Display: **Fraunces** (serif), italics for emphasis. Use for the headline
  and the sign-off.
- UI / labels: **Instrument Sans** (humanist sans). Use for captions,
  identifiers, lower-thirds.
- Mono: **JetBrains Mono**. Use for small status labels, timestamps,
  numbers.

**The brand mark:**

`RELAY•` — the word RELAY in tracked-open uppercase sans, immediately
followed by a moss-green filled circle as a baseline-period. The dot is
the brand. Animate it with a subtle pulse (2.4s, scale 1 → 1.7, opacity
0.5 → 0) when it represents an *active* moment of human contact.

**Imagery rules:**

- Real-feeling product UI: actual chat threads, real code in dark editors,
  real Zoom interfaces. Never "futuristic" mockups, never glowy AI
  hologram aesthetic.
- When a human appears, show a real first name and a real-looking person
  (or photo-realistic avatar). No corporate stock smiles. No 3D abstract
  shapes. No AI-generated faces with the obvious AI tells.
- Engineer photos: square crop, ink-and-cream toned, with a tiny green
  dot status bug on the bottom-right of the avatar.

**Motion principles:**

- The green dot pulses to mean *presence*. Reserve dot-pulse motion for
  active "a human is here" beats.
- Transitions are 200ms ease-out. No parallax, no scroll-triggered drama,
  no auto-playing video-within-video.
- Whitespace is part of the message. Frames breathe.

---

## 3. Music & sound design

- Soft electric piano, single note per beat, building over the 60s.
- One diegetic sound cue: a gentle *tick* the moment the green dot is
  pressed. Not a logo sting.
- VO: warm, mid-pitch, considered. Not American hype, not British arch.
  Dry. Closer to *Anthropic interview* than *Apple keynote*.
- Mix: dialogue at -16 LUFS, music sits 12 dB under the VO.

---

## 4. Storyboard — 8 beats, 60 seconds

### Beat 1 — 0:00–0:06 (6s) · The build moment

**Shot:** Wide, cream background. A laptop screen comes up, occupying the
center. On screen: a Lovable / Cursor-style AI build canvas. Code is
moving. A non-tech-looking builder (over-shoulder POV, hands on keyboard).

**On-screen typography (Fraunces italic, lower-third, ink):**
*A marketing manager. A founder. An analyst. Building software with AI.*

**VO:** "You're building with AI. It's working — most of the way."

### Beat 2 — 0:06–0:13 (7s) · The wall

**Shot:** Tight on the laptop screen. The AI's last reply hangs there.
The terminal shows `→ 401 Unauthorized` in soft red. Cursor blinks. A
small sigh from the builder (we hear breath, no words).

**On-screen typography (mono, top-right, ink-mute):**
`STUCK · STRIPE WEBHOOK · 401`

**VO:** "And then there's the part where the AI runs out — and you wish a
real engineer was in the room."

### Beat 3 — 0:13–0:20 (7s) · The press

**Shot:** The viewport zooms to the top-right corner of the AI tool.
A small green dot pulses gently. A thin tooltip appears in
Instrument Sans: *"Press for an engineer."* The cursor moves toward it.

**On-screen typography (display, large center, ink):**
**The press.** *(italic, deep moss)*

**VO:** "That's the press."

**Sound:** A soft *tick* on the click.

### Beat 4 — 0:20–0:30 (10s) · The arrival

**Shot:** The green dot becomes a small "card" in the corner. A photo of
a real-looking senior engineer slides in, cream-toned with a moss-green
status bug. Caption underneath, mono small: `PRIYA R. · SENIOR ENGINEER ·
JOINING…` then `JOINED · ONLINE`.

A second pane opens — a Zoom-style video tile in the bottom-right of the
laptop frame. Same engineer, friendly, calm.

**On-screen typography (Fraunces italic, lower-third):**
*By name. By face. In your session.*

**VO:** "A senior engineer joins your build. By name. By face. Through
the same Zoom your team already uses."

### Beat 5 — 0:30–0:38 (8s) · Three modalities

**Shot:** Split screen, three vertical panels with a 200ms cross-fade
between each:
1. **Chat** — text bubbles in the AI tool's sidebar, builder + engineer
   exchanging.
2. **Voice** — a waveform animating, headset icon, the builder pacing.
3. **Screen share** — the engineer's cursor moves over the builder's
   code, both cursors visible.

**On-screen typography (each panel, sans bold):**
**Chat.** **Voice.** **Screen share.** Then below: *One engineer.*

**VO:** "Chat, voice, or screen share — you pick. The session is on Zoom,
recorded with consent, tied to your project."

### Beat 6 — 0:38–0:48 (10s) · Three phases

**Shot:** A horizontal track moving right-to-left, with three station
cards. Each card has a number, a label, and a one-line role.

```
Phase 01 · Build Phase
You build. Your AI engineer supports.
```

```
Phase 02 · Launch and Go-Live
Relay leads the effort.
```

```
Phase 03 · Maintain and Scale
Relay takes accountability. You focus on your business.
```

The same engineer's avatar follows along the track, dot pulsing softly
as it passes each station.

**On-screen typography (display large, ink, after the track settles):**
*Same engineer. End to end.*

**VO:** "From building, to going live, to keeping it running — same team,
same engineer, end to end. From your first press to next week's bug."

### Beat 7 — 0:48–0:55 (7s) · Trust posture

**Shot:** Wide world map, ink background, cream dots blinking on across
timezones — New York, London, Bengaluru, Sydney, São Paulo. Beneath the
map, a mono-caps row of compliance badges fades up: `GDPR · SOC 2 ·
PER-TENANT ISOLATION · DPA AVAILABLE`.

**On-screen typography (Fraunces italic, lower-third):**
*Headquartered in New York. Engineers in fifteen-plus countries.*

**VO:** "Headquartered in New York. Engineers in fifteen-plus countries.
GDPR-aligned and built to enterprise compliance from day one."

### Beat 8 — 0:55–1:00 (5s) · End card

**Shot:** Solid cream. The RELAY wordmark animates in, letter by letter,
finishing with the green dot landing into place with a tiny scale-down
bounce. Then a single italic Fraunces line below.

```
RELAY•

Build with AI. Ship with engineers.

relay.green · Press for an engineer.
```

**VO:** "Build with AI. Ship with engineers. Relay."

**Sound:** The piano resolves to a single sustained note. Cut.

## 7. Captions & alt-cuts

- **Always-on captions baked into the master** so the cut reads silent on
  social.
- **15-second cut for paid social:** lift Beat 1 (hook) + Beat 4 (engineer
  arrival) + Beat 8 (end card).
- **6-second pre-roll:** Beat 3 (the press) into Beat 8 (end card).
- **Vertical 9:16 export** for TikTok / Reels / Shorts. Re-frame the
  Beat 6 horizontal phase track as a vertical scroll.

---

## 8. Tool-specific notes

- **Runway Gen-4:** Generate the engineer-arrival shot (Beat 4) and the
  modalities split (Beat 5) here — best for cinematic real-feel motion.
- **Pika 2.0:** Generate the laptop / code / cursor beats (1, 2, 3) —
  cheap and fast for stylized editorial cuts.
- **Sora / Veo:** Generate the world map shot (Beat 7) — handles
  geographic establishing shots well.
- **Descript:** Final timeline assembly + caption baking + audio mix.
- **AI voice (ElevenLabs / HeyGen):** Use a warm mid-pitch
  English-American voice. Example reference: the calm voice on Anthropic's
  brand films, *not* a startup launch video.

## 9. The "would a senior engineer say this out loud, with a straight face,
to another engineer?" check

Run every line in the cut through that test. If yes, ship it. If not,
rewrite. (Brand voice rule from the Relay brand guidelines.)

The lines that pass the test in this brief:

- "The AI runs out." ✓
- "Press for an engineer." ✓
- "By name, by face." ✓
- "Same engineer, end to end." ✓
- "Build with AI. Ship with engineers." ✓

The lines we did *not* use, because they fail the test:

- ~~"Revolutionize your workflow"~~
- ~~"World-class engineering at your fingertips"~~
- ~~"Unleash the power of AI with humans in the loop"~~

If a line in the generated cut comes back sounding like one of those,
strike it.
