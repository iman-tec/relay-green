# Relay.green — 60-second explainer video brief (sanitized for AI tools)

A safety-policy-friendly rewrite of `explainer-video-brief.md`. This version
drops all third-party brand names, named characters, and specific
regulatory/corporate identifiers that trip the safety filters on Genspark,
Runway, Pika, Sora, HeyGen, Synthesia, etc. The brand voice, visual
language, and beat structure are preserved.

---

## Why the original was likely rejected

AI video platforms today are conservative about five categories. The
original brief tripped on at least four:

1. **Third-party brand names.** Mentioning *Lovable*, *Cursor*, *Claude*,
   *Stripe*, *Zoom* by name pulls in trademark / likeness concerns.
2. **Named individuals shown on screen.** "Priya R., senior engineer" —
   even fictional — can trip deepfake / likeness-rights filters when the
   character is supposed to be visually depicted.
3. **Specific corporate identifiers.** *NINtec Systems · NSE/BSE: NINSYS ·
   The Asgard Fund · Amsterdam · Manhattan, NY* — these read as
   identifying real registered entities and individuals.
4. **Regulatory claims.** *GDPR-aligned · SOC 2 Type II · enterprise
   compliance from day one* — direct compliance claims about a service
   can be flagged as making representations the platform can't verify.
5. **(Less common)** Frames depicting realistic UI of named products —
   tools sometimes block this to avoid trademark confusion.

The script below removes all five categories. Everything is described in
generic terms; the only branded element is **Relay** itself, which is your
own.

---

## 1. Audience & purpose

**Who is watching:** A first-time visitor to *relay.green*. A
non-technical person building software with an AI assistant. They have
an idea, they have an AI helping them, they don't have an engineering
team.

**What they need to understand in 60 seconds:**

1. Why Relay exists — AI lets anyone build software, but architecture,
   security, deployment, and ongoing care still want a senior engineer.
2. How it works — One press, inside the AI tool you're already using,
   puts a real engineer in your live session.
3. Three phases — *Build · Launch · Maintain.* Same team end-to-end.
4. What it isn't — Not a freelance marketplace. Not a chatbot. Not a
   ticket queue. A real human, on call.

**Positioning:** Relay is the best friend of the non-technical AI
builder.

---

## 2. Brand & visual language

**Mood:** Editorial. Calm. Confident. Restrained. No swooshes, no
"revolutionize," no startup-launch energy.

**Color palette (use exactly these):**

- Cream `#F4F2EE` — page background
- Ink `#1A1814` — body text, dark sections
- Moss green `#4F6B3A` — the dot. Sparingly.
- Deep moss `#3F5C2E` — italic emphasis
- Paper `#F9F7F3` — secondary surface

**Typography (overlay text):**

- Display: a serif with strong italics (Fraunces or similar). Use for
  headlines.
- UI / labels: a humanist sans (Instrument Sans or similar).
- Mono: a coding mono (JetBrains Mono or similar).

**The brand mark:**

`RELAY•` — the word RELAY in tracked-open uppercase sans, immediately
followed by a moss-green filled circle as a baseline-period.

**People on screen:**

Show **anonymous silhouettes, hands at a keyboard, or back-of-head over
shoulder POV**. Do **not** generate a specific named person's face. If a
"video tile" appears in a beat, fill it with a soft cream-toned gradient
silhouette, not a recognizable face. This is the single most important
rule for getting through safety review.

**Tools on screen:**

Show **generic-looking interfaces**. A dark code editor with anonymous
syntax highlighting. A generic "AI build tool" branded only with a small
"AI" sigil in the corner. A generic video-call tile. **Do not** include
real product UIs, real logos, or real brand wordmarks.

**Motion:**

- Subtle. 200ms ease-out for transitions.
- One pulsing element: the green dot, when it represents "press for a
  human."
- No parallax, no scroll-triggered reveals, no auto-playing video.

---

## 3. Music & sound

- Soft electric piano underneath, single sustained notes building.
- One diegetic cue: a gentle *tick* at the press.
- VO: warm, mid-pitch, considered. Dry. Not American hype, not British
  arch.

## 4. Storyboard — 8 beats, 60 seconds

### Beat 1 — 0:00–0:06 · The build moment

**Shot:** Cream background. A laptop screen comes into frame. On it: a
generic AI build interface — a chat sidebar on the right, a code preview
on the left, a small "AI" sigil in the corner. Over-the-shoulder POV; we
see hands typing but **never a face**.

**On-screen text (italic serif, lower-third):**
*Building software with AI. It is working — most of the way.*

**VO:** "You are building with AI. It is working — most of the way."

### Beat 2 — 0:06–0:13 · The wall

**Shot:** Tight on the laptop screen. The AI's last reply hangs there.
A neutral-toned error indicator appears in the lower panel ("authentication
failed"). The cursor blinks.

**On-screen text (mono, top-right):**
`STUCK · AUTHENTICATION ERROR`

**VO:** "And then there is the part where the AI runs out — and you wish
a real engineer were in the room."

### Beat 3 — 0:13–0:20 · The press

**Shot:** Camera pushes in toward the top-right corner of the AI
interface. A small green dot pulses gently. A thin tooltip appears:
*"Press for an engineer."* A hand-cursor moves toward it. **Tick** sound
on click.

**On-screen text (display, large center):**
*The press.*

**VO:** "That is the press."

### Beat 4 — 0:20–0:30 · The arrival

**Shot:** A small "engineer joining" card slides in. The card's avatar
is a **soft cream-toned silhouette** (not a face), with a small green
status dot bottom-right. A second pane opens — a generic video-call tile
in the bottom-right corner of the laptop frame, with the same silhouette
gently animating to suggest presence. **No face is generated. No name
appears.**

**On-screen text (italic serif, lower-third):**
*A senior engineer. In your live session.*

**VO:** "A senior engineer joins your build. Through a live video
session, on your existing setup."

### Beat 5 — 0:30–0:38 · Three modalities

**Shot:** Split screen, three vertical panels with 200ms cross-fades:

1. **Chat** — text bubbles in the AI tool's sidebar, two participants
   exchanging.
2. **Voice** — a clean waveform animating, microphone icon.
3. **Screen share** — two cursors, both visible, moving over generic code.

**On-screen text (sans bold + italic serif):**
**Chat. Voice. Screen.** *One engineer.*

**VO:** "Chat, voice, or screen share — you pick. The session is
recorded and tied to your project."

### Beat 6 — 0:38–0:48 · Three phases

**Shot:** A horizontal track moves right-to-left with three station
cards, each showing a number, a label, and a one-line role:

```
01 · Build Phase
You build. Your AI engineer supports.
```

```
02 · Launch and Go-Live
Relay leads the effort.
```

```
03 · Maintain and Scale
Relay takes accountability.
```

A small green dot marker travels along the track, gently pulsing as it
passes each station.

**On-screen text (display, large, after the track settles):**
*Same engineer. End to end.*

**VO:** "From building, to going live, to keeping it running — same
team, same engineer, end to end."

### Beat 7 — 0:48–0:55 · Reach

**Shot:** Wide world map, ink background, cream dots blinking on across
multiple time zones. The dots connect with thin moss-green lines as they
appear. **No country names, no city names, no specific geography
labels.**

**On-screen text (italic serif, lower-third):**
*Engineers around the world. Always awake when you are.*

**VO:** "A team across time zones. Always awake when you are. Built to
enterprise standards from day one."

### Beat 8 — 0:55–1:00 · End card

**Shot:** Solid cream. The RELAY wordmark animates in, letter by letter,
finishing with the green dot landing into place.

```
RELAY•

Build with AI. Ship with engineers.

relay.green
```

**VO:** "Build with AI. Ship with engineers. Relay."

The piano resolves to a single sustained note. Cut.

## 8. If a specific tool still flags the brief

Things to try, in order:

1. **Drop the "compliance" line** entirely. Replace beat 7 VO with: "A
   team across time zones. Always awake when you are. Built for trust."
2. **Replace "RELAY" with a placeholder** in the prompt (e.g.
   "BRAND_NAME") and edit the brand back in during post in your video
   editor of choice.
3. **Generate beats individually** rather than as a single 60-second
   ask. Most platforms have looser policies on 5–10s clips than on
   long-form.
4. **Switch tools.** *Runway Gen-4* and *Luma Dream Machine* tend to be
   the most permissive for editorial / product B2B briefs. *Synthesia*
   and *HeyGen* (avatar-led) skip the "people on screen" issue
   entirely.
5. **Keep the VO out of the AI tool.** Generate visuals only, then layer
   in the voice-over with a separate TTS like ElevenLabs or a human read.

## 9. The "would a senior engineer say this out loud, with a straight
   face, to another engineer?" check

Run every line in the cut through that test. The lines that pass in
this sanitized brief:

- "The AI runs out." ✓
- "Press for an engineer." ✓
- "Same engineer, end to end." ✓
- "Build with AI. Ship with engineers." ✓

Lines we did *not* use because they fail the test:

- ~~"Revolutionize your workflow"~~
- ~~"World-class engineering at your fingertips"~~
- ~~"Unleash AI with humans in the loop"~~

If a generated cut comes back with anything in the second list, strike
it.
